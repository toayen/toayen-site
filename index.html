Questions

How much does it cost?

Toayen is free to download and free to use for planning, tracking and sharing. Premium adds more personalised suggestions, full spending insights and occasion budgets, at £2.99 a month or £17.99 a year.

Do I need an account?

No. Toayen works without signing in. Signing in with Apple is optional, and only needed for personalised suggestions and to sync across your devices.

Where is my data kept?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud if you turn on sync. The people you add, your notes and your photos are yours. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Does it read my contacts?

Only if you choose to import someone, and only the person you pick. Nothing is uploaded — contact details you import stay on your device.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Toayen is currently iPhone and iPad only.
