your swipe is your swing

There are no power meters to stop and no buttons to time. You draw your swing on the pad with one finger, and Shotmaker reads it the way a launch monitor reads a real one. The ball then flies through real air, with real drag and spin.

speed

how far

A faster swipe means more club speed and a longer shot. A slower, smoother one adds loft and control.

line and face

which way

Swipe across the line to curve the ball. A draw round the dogleg or a fade into the wind is in your finger.

rhythm

how pure

A smooth, unhurried swing strikes it pure. Rush it and you will feel the difference.

no luck in it

0

There are no random numbers anywhere in the swing or the flight. Every miss is yours, and so is every good shot.

28 to +5

Shotmaker spans the range from a 28 handicap to a plus 5. New players hit it shorter and more crooked, and tour numbers take real practice.

fixed

Every player plays a handicap round in the same fixed conditions, so a handicap in Shotmaker means the same thing for everyone.

A parkland classic of rolling fairways, still lakes and old woods. Larchmere is where every player starts, and it rewards a well-placed drive more than a long one. Trees play exactly as they are drawn, and the lakes catch anything careless.

dunmarra links

A wild seaside links on a bright, breezy afternoon. Firm, fast turf runs over old dunes, deep pot bunkers hide from the tee and gorse waits beside the landing areas. The wind and the ground decide the shot, so the ball has to be kept low and allowed to run.

cormorant point

Three landscapes in one round. Cormorant Point starts in a forest of tall dark pines, breaks out over white dunes and ice plant, then runs along the clifftops above turquoise coves. Its white bunkers are torn into capes and bays, and on the run home the ball is carried over the ocean itself.

cinder mesa

A clear morning in red-rock desert. Emerald fairways run as ribbons through red sand and sagebrush, past sandstone domes and across a field of black lava. The last holes drop through canyons to a creek below the clubhouse. The air is still, so this is target golf where only the carry counts.

glacier falls

A summer's day in a mountain valley. Striped fairways roll between walls of dark spruce, a milky turquoise river runs beside them, and limestone peaks fill the sky. everything a golfer wants

a real handicap

Watch it fall as you improve, with every round's scorecard kept.

stats that help

See your fairways, greens, putts, up and downs, sand saves and the carry of every club in your bag.

a caddie

The caddie reads the hole and suggests the play. The shot is still yours to hit.

a practice ground

Use the range with targets, or the putting, chipping and pitching greens.

eleven lessons

Learn rhythm, distance, shape, trajectory, the short game, reading greens and playing the wind.

your phone only

There is no account and no tracking. Your rounds stay on your phone.
