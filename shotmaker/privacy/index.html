privacy policy

shotmaker privacy

Last updated [date of publication]

Shotmaker is a golf game by Toayen. We built it to keep your game on your phone, and we do not collect personal information.

what we collect

Nothing. Shotmaker has no account, no advertising, no analytics service and no tracking. It does not send your swings, scores or handicap anywhere.

what stays on your phone

Your swings, rounds, scorecards, handicap, practice records and settings are stored on your device only. They are included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on, under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes them.

purchases

Purchases and subscriptions (the Shotmaker Club, or a course bought on its own) are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not see your name, email address or payment details. The app only checks with Apple which courses you are entitled to play.

crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings (Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. These contain no personal information, and you can turn sharing off at any time in the same place.

children

Shotmaker is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

changes

If Shotmaker ever adds features that need data to leave your phone, such as online leaderboards, we will update this policy before they are used and say plainly what is sent and why.

contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Toayen at info@toayen.co.uk.

This policy covers Shotmaker only. The Toayen gift-planning app has its own privacy policy.
