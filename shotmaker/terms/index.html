The game

Shotmaker is a golf game for iPhone. Larchmere and Dunmarra Links are free to play for ever. Every other course is in the Shotmaker Club, or can be bought on its own and kept.

Courses you buy

A course bought on its own is yours to play for as long as Shotmaker is available on your Apple Account, on any iPhone signed in to it. Use Restore purchases on the Club page if a new phone does not show it.

Your handicap

A Shotmaker handicap is the game's own measure of how well you play Shotmaker. It is not an official golf handicap and cannot be used as one. Every handicap round is played in the same fixed conditions for every player, and nothing bought in the game changes how the ball flies.

Your rounds are recorded on your phone with every swing, so that a round can be played again exactly and checked. Altering the app or its files to change a round or a handicap is not allowed.

Matches with friends, the weekly tournament and the leaderboards need a Game Center account, which is Apple's and under Apple's terms. Everything else in Shotmaker plays without one. Our privacy policy says what goes to our server and how to delete it.

  • The weekly tournament is free to enter and has no prize. Its rounds do not count towards your handicap. Every player may play its course, whether or not they have the Club.

A round that does not match its record, or that was played with an altered app, may be taken off the boards, and a player who cheats may be barred from online play. If you think a decision about your round is wrong, write to us and we will look again.
