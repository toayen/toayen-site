The game

Shotmaker is a golf game for iPhone and iPad. Larchmere and Dunmarra Links are free to play for ever. Every other course is in the Shotmaker Club, or can be bought on its own and kept.

A course bought on its own is yours to play for as long as Shotmaker is available on your Apple Account, on any iPhone or iPad signed in to it. Purchases are not shared through Family Sharing. Use Restore purchases on the Club page if a new phone does not show it.

Your handicap

A Shotmaker handicap is the game's own measure of how well you play Shotmaker. It is not an official golf handicap and cannot be used as one. Every handicap round is played in the same fixed conditions for every player, and nothing bought in the game changes how the ball flies.

Your rounds are recorded on your phone with every swing, so that a round can be played again exactly and checked. Altering the app or its files to change a round or a handicap is not allowed.
