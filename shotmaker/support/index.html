help with shotmaker

If your question is not answered here, email us at info@toayen.co.uk and we will reply as soon as we can.

playing

how do I swing?

Put one finger on the pad at the foot of the screen, draw it back and then swing it through. The speed of the swipe sets the distance, and its line and the face you present set the direction and the curve. The first time you play, Shotmaker asks for ten swings on the range to learn your natural swing.

why does the same swing always give the same shot?

Shotmaker has no random numbers in the swing or the flight. If you hit the same swing twice, you hit the same shot twice. That way every improvement you see is real.

can I make the game easier?

There is no difficulty setting. Handicap rounds are played in the same conditions for every player, so a handicap means the same for everyone. The settings change practice only: practice rounds, single holes and the range.

how is my handicap worked out?

Your handicap comes from your finished handicap rounds of nine or eighteen holes. You can see it, its chart and every round's scorecard on the handicap page.

is it an official handicap?

No. A Shotmaker handicap measures your game in Shotmaker only. It is not an official golf handicap and cannot be used at a real club.

does the caddie play shots for me?

No. The caddie reads the hole and suggests a club and a target, but it never plays a shot. Every shot is your own swing.

can I stop a round part way?

Yes. Shotmaker saves your round as you play. Tap Resume your round on the home screen to carry on where you left off.

do I need an internet connection?

No. You can play every course you have without a connection. You need one only to buy the Club or a course, or to restore your purchases.

the club and purchases

which courses are free?

Larchmere and Dunmarra Links are free for ever. Cormorant Point, Cinder Mesa and Glacier Falls are in the Shotmaker Club, and each can also be bought on its own to keep.

how do I restore my purchases?

Open Shotmaker Club on the home screen and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

how do I cancel the club?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Shotmaker Club. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep the Club courses until the end of the period you have paid for.

can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

your rounds

where are my rounds kept?

Your rounds, scorecards, handicap and settings are kept on your iPhone only. They are included in your iPhone's own backup if you have one turned on.

how do I move my rounds to a new iPhone?

Set up the new iPhone from a backup of the old one, using iCloud or your computer. Your Shotmaker rounds come with it.

what happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Shotmaker deletes your rounds, unless they are in a backup you restore later. Your purchases are not lost, because they belong to your Apple Account.
