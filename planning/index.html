Gift planning

Most of the difficulty in giving good presents is not choosing them. It is that the information you need arrives months before you need it, in the middle of a conversation about something else, and by December it has gone.

These articles are about that gap. How to hold on to what people say, what to do about the person who seems to have everything, and how to set a budget that still means something on the 20th of December.

They are written from the same view: that a well-chosen present usually costs less than a hurried one, and that the difference is almost always something you already knew and forgot.

Toayen is the app that came out of the same problem, and it is now free on the App Store.
